FCM v0.9.0 - Automated Data Checks (TEST; not yet Windows-verified)
===================================================================

Data providers are INDEPENDENT:
- AHledger price tables: 30 minutes by default, or 60 minutes. Two requests
  per run for Alliance and Horde, on the selected ruleset/region. Does not
  make ForeverDB API requests. Prices are provider-supplied market snapshots.
- ForeverDB build check: every 12 hours by default, or every 24 hours.
  Only requests build metadata with your private, user-encrypted key.
  A change is recorded in fdb-build-status.json; FULL catalog downloads are
  still performed through the regular Windows Data Companion by the user.
  This is intentional until safe unattended imports are validated on Windows.
- Character scans: in the WoW addon after login, not related to provider APIs.

INSTALL ONCE, after you install the TEST version of FCM and save your approved
ForeverDB key through the Data Companion:
  Open ...\Interface\AddOns\ForeverCharacterManager\FCM-Companion
  Enter powershell in File Explorer's location bar.
  Execute:
  powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Enable-FCM-ScheduledRefresh.ps1 -AuctionMinutes 30 -ForeverDBHours 12 -RunNow

For 60-minute AH prices and once-daily ForeverDB checks:
  powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Enable-FCM-ScheduledRefresh.ps1 -AuctionMinutes 60 -ForeverDBHours 24 -RunNow

To disable both:
  powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Disable-FCM-ScheduledRefresh.ps1

NOTES
- These jobs are not installed until YOU execute the enable script on Windows.
- PC must be powered on, account signed in, internet available. Scheduled
  triggers are renewed when the setup script runs again (10-year duration).
- The current-price cache honors the provider's Cache-Control, and may expire
  BEFORE the next scheduled update. A new fetch does not guarantee a new AH
  listing or data from an unscanned market. AHledger's own sync can supply
  its addon with market data more continuously.
- Reload or relog in WoW to load externally generated price data.
- Do NOT publish your ForeverDB personal key, DPAPI key file, settings.json,
  reference-cache.json or in-game saved character data on GitHub/CurseForge.
- A ForeverDB key exemption is required for your local Windows companion and
  must be renewed every 90 days. Free tier: 5000/day, 30/min.
- Attribution: Data from ForeverDB.net — https://foreverdb.net
  Prices from AHledger — https://ahledger.com
- The Windows companion does not remotely alter characters or spend gold.
- WINDOWS TASK REGISTRATION AND LIVE SERVER TESTING STILL REQUIRED.
