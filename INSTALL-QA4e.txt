FCM v0.9.1 QA4e — Round gathering markers and treasure chests
=============================================================

PREVIEW — not published to GitHub; live WoW Forever testing still required.

1. Fully close World of Warcraft Forever and the FCM Windows companion.
2. Back up the installed ForeverCharacterManager addon directory and your
   WoW WTF/Account SavedVariables before updating.
3. Extract FCM-v0.9.1-QA4e-Round-Pins-Treasure.zip.
4. Copy the extracted ForeverCharacterManager directory into:

   C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\

   Choose to merge folders and replace files with the same names. Do not
   create nested ForeverCharacterManager/ForeverCharacterManager directories.
   DO NOT delete the installed addon directory. Your companion's encrypted
   key, local reference cache, generated data, settings and WoW SavedVariables
   must not be removed. None are contained in the ZIP.

5. Check the installed build ID in Windows PowerShell:

   Select-String -Path "C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\ForeverCharacterManager\Core.lua" -Pattern "FCM.build"

   Expected: QA4e-20261114-Round-Pins-Treasure

6. Restart the game. Use /fcm > Gathering, Pins: On, with Hide within: 15 yd
   (recommended starting distance). Saved herbs and ore use circular icon
   masks if the game client supports them. Their item art remains distinct.
7. Open a real world treasure chest after hovering its name. FCM should
   record the chest following a loot event. Open /fcm > Gathering to inspect.
   Some Forever clients may not expose the necessary chest names or masking
   API; see the QA4 testing notes for fallback and exact checks.

The large FCM launcher button remains unchanged. Saved node icons disappear
when nearby according to your adjustable Hide within distance, leaving room
for native WoW tracking dots. Treasure points are saved at the character's
position at the time of looting, not a guaranteed exact chest location.
