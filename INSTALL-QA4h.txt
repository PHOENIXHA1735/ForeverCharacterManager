FCM v0.9.1 QA4h — book-style minimap rim icons
Build: QA4h-20261114-Rim-Book-Style

SAFE WINDOWS UPDATE
1. Fully close World of Warcraft: Forever and FCM's Windows companion.
2. Back up your installed ForeverCharacterManager folder and WTF SavedVariables.
3. Open the ZIP. Copy its ForeverCharacterManager folder into:
   C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\
4. MERGE the folders and replace matching addon source files when prompted.
   Do NOT delete the existing folder. Do NOT remove your saved data, generated
   reference files, community cache, encrypted API key or companion cache.
5. Start WoW. FCM Diagnostics should show:
   QA4h-20261114-Rim-Book-Style

Optional PowerShell verification (PowerShell 5.1 is fine):
Select-String -Path "C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\ForeverCharacterManager\Core.lua" -Pattern "FCM.build"

LIVE TEST
1. Open /fcm > Gathering, set Pins: On; Rim beyond: 45 yd;
   Hide within: 15 yd; Size: 16 px.
2. Saved gathering icons beyond 45 yards should sit directly ON the curved
   minimap rim like its book markers (about 12 px in diameter), with half
   their art inside and half outside. In-map nearby pins remain 16 px.
3. At 45 yards, a pin should move inside the map; at 15 yards, hide to
   reveal the native gathering dot. Previously gathered nodes remain saved.
4. Choose Size: 24 px. Rim pins should be roughly 18 px and still centered
   on the rim. Try /fcm gatherpins size 19 for a custom whole-pixel size.
5. Hover a pin to confirm resource name and personal gather count. Rotate
   and zoom the minimap; record any positioning issues with a screenshot.

The FCM launcher icon and built-in book symbols are not resized or moved.
