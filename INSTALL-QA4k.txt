5. Restart WoW; open /fcm > Diagnostics and verify the build above.

TEST THE WORLD MAP
1. On Spotty Dotty, confirm FCM > Gathering > World Map: On.
2. Open Teldrassil with M. Previously saved herbs should now draw over
   the terrain, including those outside your minimap radius.
3. Hover an icon to check the saved node name and gather count. Pan or zoom
   to confirm icons remain anchored to their map locations.
4. Close M, open FCM > Diagnostics, and select FCM System Health.
   The `Gathering world map:` line now keeps the last open-map result even
   after the map closes and includes `pins drawn` / `first visible`.
5. If no icons appear, send that diagnostic line and a world-map screenshot.
