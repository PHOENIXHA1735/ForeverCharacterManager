FOREVER CHARACTER MANAGER v0.8.9 — INSTALLATION

1. Fully exit WoW Forever and the FCM Data Companion.
2. Back up your addon directory and account WTF SavedVariables.
3. Extract the ForeverCharacterManager folder under Interface\AddOns, replacing existing addon files.
4. KEEP ExternalData.generated.lua and SavedVariables; they are not included in this package.
5. Restart WoW and confirm FCM v0.8.9 in the title header.
6. Open Frosty > Gear > Novice Ardent's Sash. Verify stats, textures, explanation, score, and material requirements.
