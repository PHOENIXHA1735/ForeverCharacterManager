The Icy Veins pages expose priorities and individual spell coefficients. A spell coefficient is not a marginal whole-character gear weight. Several endgame pages explicitly describe beta/theorycraft uncertainty. No universally applicable, build-validated numerical catalog was accepted for FCM. This does not claim that no numerical simulations exist.

Cross-checks: [Method Mage leveling](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-mage-leveling-guide-and-talents), Celz, updated 2026-11-14; [Mobalytics Mage leveling](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide), Asuna_qt, updated 2026-11-04. Both are Forever leveling sources. Mobalytics emphasizes Spell Power/Intellect and situational Stamina, rather than supplying transferable numerical weights; this differs in emphasis from the Icy Veins Frost order and must not be hidden. Method corroborates mana dependence/survival context, not a gear coefficient table.

[WoWSims Forever](https://github.com/wowsims/forever) is a primary-source simulation project. Its existence was verified; its outputs were not validated for this installed client build, exact talents, level, equipment, target and rotation. No static EP defaults were imported as universal weights. Generic editorial/Classic-adjusted calculators and other game versions were excluded.

Contextual caps: Icy Veins caster endgame pages describe 17% total spell hit versus level-63 bosses; the Frost leveling page discusses same-level targets separately. Warrior pages distinguish 8% specials/weapon-and-shield or two-handed attacks from 27% dual-wield white attacks, before talent bonuses, and 6.5% dodge reduction from behind versus 14% parry reduction from the front. Protection/Feral source pages discuss 440 total Defense. These are sourced reference contexts, not automatic item-rating caps or conversions; no numeric caps were injected into FCM without a validated character model.

Known source caveats: Survival Hunter leveling retains ranged/beta guidance while its endgame PvE source is a melee guide. Healing priorities do not establish healer damage weights. Retribution endgame has different capstone builds. Feral and Protection Warrior have different threat/mitigation lists. Stat guidance is displayed with these distinctions. Spell Piercing is kept distinct from resistance penetration; the client capture for Piercing is not yet verified.

PvP cross-check: [Hydra's Discipline PvP guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-pvp-guide), Hydramist, updated 2026-11-15. Its early Stamina/Intellect emphasis and later healing/mana-sustain guidance are included as a separate Discipline-only ordinal reference. It does not validate an exact level bracket or numerical scale. Other specs show an explicit PvP coverage gap rather than silently using PvE guidance.
